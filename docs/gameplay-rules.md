# How to play

**Submarine** is about defending your submarine and destroying enough targets to clear each stage.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Arrow keys or `W A S D` | Move left, right, up or down |
| `Space` | Fire a missile |
| `X` | Fire a torpedo, once unlocked |
| `Esc` | Pause / menu |

On touchscreens, use the on-screen direction pad, **MISSILE** and **TORPEDO** buttons. The separate **PAUSE / MENU** button freezes the game and offers **Continue** or **Main Menu**. Choose **Classic** or **Modern** with a tap on the title screen. Hold left or right to move across multiple positions. Landscape orientation provides a larger display.

Your submarine moves between **six positions** across the screen and **two depths** underwater.

## Weapons and enemies

- **Bombs** that fall straight down use the round symbol. Angling bombs use a filled torpedo-shaped symbol so their different path is easier to recognise; they do not home in on the submarine.
- **Missiles** fire straight up. They destroy aircraft and surface ships, and can shoot down falling bombs and depth charges.
- **Torpedoes** travel sideways and destroy enemy submarines. They are available only in the submarine stage (**1-3**, **2-3**, and so on) and can also intercept enemy torpedoes.
- You can have **one missile and one torpedo in flight at once**. Each must finish its journey before you can fire another of the same kind.
- Aircraft may drop bombs, surface ships drop depth charges, and enemy submarines fire torpedoes. Some aircraft and enemy submarines pass through without attacking.

In **Classic**, torpedoes aim left or right toward a visible enemy submarine. If none is present, they travel in the direction you last faced. In **Modern**, they always use your last facing direction.

## Stages and scoring

| Stage | Your objective |
| --- | --- |
| **1-1 — Air Attack** | Destroy 8 aircraft |
| **1-2 — Surface Sweepers** | Destroy 4 surface ships |
| **1-3 — Enemy Submarines** | Destroy 4 enemy submarines |

Each stage adds a new enemy type while keeping the earlier threats. Torpedoes are locked again when the next cycle starts. Only the stage's designated enemy advances your progress, but destroying any enemy earns points. Each successful interception also earns 10 points. You recover one lost submarine every 10,000 points, up to three lives.

After stage 1-3, the stages repeat at a faster pace with higher targets.

## Classic or Modern?

**Classic** plays in distinct, handheld-style steps. Aircraft change between fixed flight attitudes.

**Modern** keeps the same submarine controls and stages, but enemies move smoothly and vary their speed and altitude.

## Lives and game over

You start with **three submarines**. Each loss lights an X on the display. A replacement submarine enters and you retry the same stage while keeping your score.

After the third loss, the game ends. Press **Esc** to open the pause/menu panel, then choose **Main Menu**. The high score is remembered while the page remains open.
